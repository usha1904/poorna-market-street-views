Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Market

360° Render Perspectives

The revitalized Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (Poorna) Market seamlessly blends colonial-era heritage with forward-looking sustainable design. The restored clock tower and landmark facade anchor the urban precinct, replacing congested lanes with shaded pedestrian promenades, modular weather-resilient vendor stalls, an open-air stepped amphitheatre, and landscaped civic plazas that harmonize past culture with contemporary civic life.
