Poorna Market 360 Deployment Guide

Hosting on GitHub Pages & Viewing inside Meta Quest

Step 1: Adding your Enscape Panorama to GitHub

  1. Save your 360 image with the filename: poorna-market-view.jpg
  2. In your GitHub repo: usha1904/poorna-market-street-views
  3. Click Add file > Upload files
  4. Drag poorna-market-view.jpg into the root folder (beside `index.html`) and commit.
  5. The viewer automatically detects and renders this image on reload!

Step 2: Entering VR in Meta Quest 2 / Quest 3

  • Put on your Meta Quest headset.
  • Open the pre-installed Meta Quest Browser.
  • Visit: https://usha1904.github.io/poorna-market-street-views/
  • Tap the orange ENTER VR button in the top right.
  • You will enter full 6DoF stereoscopic immersion with true architectural scale.
